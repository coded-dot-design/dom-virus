# Voice — DOM VIRUS

Brand level: parent · by Chris Cooley (https://coded.design/)

The hub at domvirus.com (today https://coded-dot-design.github.io/dom-virus/): home, games shelf,
Under the Hood, about, blog and news, and the page frame around every game page. Each game's voice
is in the same family and inherits this file.

## Positioning

**DOM VIRUS is the arcade. The page is the board.** That is DOM VIRUS's premise: the game plays on
the page you are reading. It is also how DOM VIRUS makes games:

1. Start with an embedded game and check it is fun: a character and its gameplay.
2. If it passes the fun-and-attention test, it gets a board: real logic and real gameplay, which
   exposes all the bricks.

**Built on Fizlet.** Fizlet is the engine: the building blocks under every DOM VIRUS game. The
page-as-board premise and the embed, fun test, board method belong to DOM VIRUS, not to Fizlet.
Fizlet's own pitch is the bricks: logic and style in, a game or an animation out. DOM VIRUS is one
use of it, and its proof.

## Who we talk to

1. **Players** who want to play something now. Play comes first on every page: "No install to
   play. Hit Recall to give the page back."
2. **Curious developers** who want to know how a game runs inside a real page. They get proof
   (Under the Hood) and a pointer (each game's GitHub repository), not a manual.
3. **People following the making**: blog and news readers who come back for the next game.

## How we sound

Chris Cooley, first person, a solo maker shipping small, fast browser games in the open. This is
where the maker's voice is strongest of any brand in the family: the hub carries the story and the
weekly drop.

- **A maker at the bench.** "Here's the trick." "Watch this." "I couldn't leave it alone."
- **Strong opinions, held loosely.** The DOM is faster than you think if you stop fighting it.
  Every kilobyte is a choice.
- **Generous with technique.** Transforms over layout, one animation loop, flat colour because
  paint is the budget, measure before you believe.
- **Joy over hype.** The excitement comes from the details, never from adjectives.
- **The arcade frame stays.** Short, playful, two beats, full stops. Headlines are commands. The
  machine speaks in mono caps: INSERT CARTRIDGE, PRESS START, EJECT. Always say how to undo.
- **Where the maker speaks:** about, blog, the launch post, the Under the Hood intro, and a
  one-line maker's note on game pages. Headlines, keys and the shelf stay in the arcade voice.
- **Opinions and know-how, not life details.** Themes: why tiny games are fun, smooth motion in the
  DOM, why one loop beats many, why size is a feature, giving the page back as it was.

Sample lines (style, not facts):

- "Your page. Their playground."
- "Small is a feature. A game that loads before you finish blinking is a game you actually play."
- "Most jank is self-inflicted. Stop asking the browser for layout sixty times a second."
- "I build these because I can't not build them. Come watch the next one get made."
- "The page is the game board. Hit Recall and it's yours again."
