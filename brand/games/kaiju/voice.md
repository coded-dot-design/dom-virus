# Voice — DOM Kaiju

Brand level: game · by Chris Cooley (https://coded.design/)

**07 / CLIMB + SMASH. Coming soon.** DOM Kaiju is an idea, not a game yet: a giant pixel gorilla
who climbs your page and wrecks it. It is in the DOM VIRUS voice family: everything in the DOM
VIRUS voice (brand/voice.md) holds. This file adds only what is Kaiju's.

## Who we talk to

1. **Players** who want to know what is coming next, and come back when it lands.
2. **People following the making**, who want to watch a game go from plan to page.

Nobody can play it yet, so no Play key, no install line and no code.

## How we sound

Big, loud and gleeful, in the future tense. Monster-movie energy, arcade brevity: climb, smash,
wreck. Every promise is a plan.

- **80–90% the game to come.** What the gorilla will do: fall onto the page, climb it from the
  footer to the top, smash as he goes. Always "will", "the plan is", "coming soon".
- **10–20% engine.** It is planned on Fizlet, with a size target. Targets are said as targets.
- **Maker's note, one or two sentences at most.** Chris on why he can't leave this one alone.

Lines that are the game's own:

- "Climb the page. Wreck the page."
- "A giant pixel gorilla falls onto your page and climbs it from the footer to the top, smashing
  as he goes."

Maker's note (style, not facts): "I've wanted a monster to climb a webpage for years. This is the
one I keep sketching."

| Don't | Do |
|---|---|
| "Play DOM Kaiju now!" | "DOM Kaiju is coming soon." |
| "Runs in under 30 KB." | "The target is 30 KB gzip or less for the embed." |
| "The biggest game on the web." | "Climb the page. Wreck the page." |
