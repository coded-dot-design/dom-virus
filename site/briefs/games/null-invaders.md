Play it here, then make it part of your own project. Visit GitHub for the source code, setup instructions, and integration examples.

[features steps]
eyebrow: HOW TO PLAY
heading: How to play Null Invaders
item: Press Play | The interceptor launches over this page. Buttons become alien factories, and the headings and paragraphs are the battlefield.
item: Fly and fire | Steer with the mouse, WASD or the arrow keys, and hold click or Space to fire. On a phone the thumbstick flies and the round Fire key shoots; hold both together.
item: Upgrade your gun | Combat XP upgrades the weapon during the run. Pulse starts with one shot; Twin unlocks at 8 XP, Trident at 24 and four-shot Overdrive at 50.
item: Break the factories | Shoot a button until its health level drains. A destroyed factory stops spawning for the rest of that wave; factories reactivate between waves.
item: Clear four waves | You have three shields. Finish wave four to clear the page. Press Escape or Recall at any time to give the page back.
[/features]

[features grid]
eyebrow: TIPS
heading: Tips
item: Shoot the text | A letter gives 1 XP and keeps the three-second combo window open, so page text is the quickest way to the next weapon tier.
item: Chain your kills | Three chained alien kills raise the combo multiplier, up to ×5. Losing a shield clears the combo but keeps your weapon.
item: Hit the factories | A factory hit gives 2 XP and a destroyed factory another 8, and it shuts that spawn source for the wave.
item: Keep moving | The squid is the fastest alien shape, and alien health rises with the wave. G jumps to the next factory; P pauses.
[/features]

[faq]
Q: Do I need to install anything to play Null Invaders?
A: No. Null Invaders plays on this page in your browser. Press Play, and press Escape or Recall when you are done to give the page back.
Q: Can I play Null Invaders on a phone?
A: Yes. Phones get a thumbstick to fly and a round Fire key, and you can hold both at once.
Q: What happens to the page when I play?
A: Shots write a literal 0 or 1 into the page's own text, and buttons become alien factories. Recall restores what the game changed and still owns.
Q: Can I put Null Invaders on my own site?
A: Yes. It is open source (MIT). The GitHub repository has the source code, setup instructions and integration examples.
[/faq]
