[features steps]
eyebrow: HOW TO PLAY
heading: How to play Sherman Bytes
item: Drive and aim | WASD or the arrows drive; the mouse aims the turret. Keyboard only, the turret follows the hull. On phones, a joystick drives and a round Fire key shoots.
item: Breach the bases | Buttons are bases that spawn tanks. Click or Space fires. Shoot a base down, then sit on it for 0.7 seconds to capture it.
item: Hold what you take | Captured bases stop spawning, repair your armor while you occupy them, and defend against nearby tanks.
item: Build | B builds cover (2 scrap), T a pillbox (8) and M a mine (3). Aim at clear ground; a blocked job refunds its cost.
item: Survive the waves | Siege waves arrive in squads, with an eight-second build break after each wave you clear. Zero armor ends the run.
[/features]

[features rows]
eyebrow: MODES
heading: Sherman Bytes modes
item: Siege | The mode on this page: waves of squads, a build break between them, and reinforcements from the viewport edges once you hold every base.
item: Conquest | The original capture-all victory condition. Choose it when you put Sherman Bytes on your own site.
[/features]

[features grid]
eyebrow: TIPS
heading: Tips
item: Letters are cover | Letters block tanks and shells. Blast gaps into faint binary rubble you can drive across.
item: Leave firing lanes | Your own cover blocks your shots too.
item: Spend your scrap | You start with 16 scrap and earn more from combat, clearing text, captured bases and finished waves.
item: Find the next base | G jumps to a hostile base and P pauses. XP upgrades the cannon at 20 and 60.
[/features]

[faq]
Q: Do I need to install anything to play?
A: No. Sherman Bytes plays on this page in your browser. Press Play, and hit Recall when you are done to give the page back.
Q: Can I play Sherman Bytes on a phone?
A: Yes. A joystick drives, the round Fire key shoots, and the Cover, Pillbox and Mine keys build.
Q: Can I put Sherman Bytes on my own site?
A: Yes. It is open source (MIT). The GitHub repository has the source code, setup instructions and integration examples.
Q: What happens to the page when I play?
A: Letters block tanks and shells, and direct hits turn nearby text into faint binary rubble. Recall gives back what the game changed and still owns.
[/faq]
