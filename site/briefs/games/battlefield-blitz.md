[features steps]
eyebrow: HOW TO PLAY
heading: How to play Battlefield Blitz
item: Pick a mode | Practice, Solo or Platoon on the start screen (it remembers your choice), or open the two-player lobby.
item: Drive and shoot | WASD or the arrows drive, the mouse aims, click or Space fires. Auto-aim starts on: Q toggles it and T locks onto one target.
item: Use the long gun | E or right-click fires the LONG gun: it locks on, then a guided round crosses the field, punches through cover and wrecks in one hit. You start with 3 long shots.
item: Call airstrikes | Press F, then click where the bombs go. A bomber flies in over your shoulder.
item: Grab tiles and win | Lit tiles give rapid fire, triple shot, shields, health, long shots and strikes; mines blow up whoever drives onto them. Most kills when the three minutes are up wins.
[/features]

[features grid]
eyebrow: CONTROLS
heading: Controls
item: Desktop | WASD or arrows drive, the mouse aims, click or Space fires. E or right-click fires a long shot, F calls an airstrike on the next click, Q is auto-aim, T aim lock and Esc pauses.
item: Phone | Drag on the left half to drive and tap the field to shoot there. AUTO and LOCK ride on FIRE; LONG and STRIKE sit in a row above it.
item: Move the keys | The grab bar on top moves the whole pad. It is remembered, and a double-click puts it back.
item: Pause | Esc or the white flag: KEEP FIGHTING or SURRENDER.
[/features]

[features rows]
eyebrow: MODES
heading: Battlefield Blitz modes
item: Practice | No clock. Three TARGET tanks that stand still and three MOVERs that drive about, none of which shoot back, while a coach walks you through every control. Ammo and strikes never run out.
item: Solo | Eight tanks, every tank for itself. Most kills in three minutes wins.
item: Platoon | You and three wingmen in your colour against two other platoons of four. No friendly fire; the platoon with the most kills wins.
item: Two players | Two people, one match, each on their own screen. Join with an invite code and a room, send the link to player 2, and bots fill the other seats.
[/features]

[features grid]
eyebrow: TIPS
heading: Tips
item: Watch the heat | The cannon fires fast but overheats if you hold it: watch the ring on FIRE.
item: Kill streaks pay | Kills in one life: 2 give a long shot, 3 an airstrike, 5 a carpet bomb and 8 a NUKE. A carpet bomb makes your next strike drop seven bombs.
item: Kills patch armour | Every kill patches 1 armour.
item: Chase the crates | Supply crates parachute in every 20 seconds or so: a medkit, long ammo, an airstrike or the jackpot.
[/features]

[features rows]
eyebrow: STRATEGY
heading: Battlefield Blitz strategy
item: Stay alive to build a streak | Streaks count kills in one life: 2 earn a long shot, 3 an airstrike, 5 a carpet bomb and 8 a NUKE that wrecks every other tank on the field. Living longer is worth more than one risky kill.
item: Save the long gun for the hard target | The long shot locks on, crosses the whole field, punches through cover and wrecks in one hit. You start with 3; Long tiles, supply crates and a 2-kill streak give more.
item: Grab Rapid to beat the heat | The cannon overheats if you hold it. A Rapid tile gives 8 seconds of rapid fire with no overheating, and Triple gives 8 seconds of triple shot.
item: Fight hurt, heal by winning | Every kill patches 1 armour and a Health tile adds 2. A Shield tile covers you for 6 seconds: take it before you push into a crowd.
item: Mind the mines | A Mine tile blows up whoever drives onto it, and goes off by itself if it is left alone. Drive around it, or lure a chaser over it.
item: Lead your platoon | In Platoon, your three wingmen hold a slot behind you, go after whoever is shooting at you and parachute back in next to you. Enemy platoons stick with their lead tank.
[/features]

[faq]
Q: How do two players play Battlefield Blitz?
A: Open the two-player page on both devices and join the same room with the same invite code, or send the link from the lobby. After the countdown you play one match; bots fill the other seats.
Q: Is Battlefield Blitz free to play?
A: Yes. It plays in your browser with no install, and it is open source (MIT).
Q: Can I put Battlefield Blitz on my own site?
A: Yes. One script tag adds a PLAY BLITZ key to any page, and the page itself becomes the arena. Escape gives it back exactly as it was.
Q: How is it related to DOM Battlefield?
A: It is the arcade version of DOM Battlefield, with the same Battlefield brand and Board UI keys. It runs on the Fizlet engine and draws Sherman Bytes' tank sprites.
[/faq]
