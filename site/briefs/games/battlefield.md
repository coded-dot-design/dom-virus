[features steps]
eyebrow: HOW TO PLAY
heading: How to play DOM Battlefield
item: Pick the ground | Choose one of four operations, a terrain density and your side, or press Quick start and anything you have not picked is rolled at random.
item: Plan the battle | Time is frozen. Set your formation and a squad of 4, 8 or 12 tanks. Fill a four-slot scout, medium and heavy loadout within 12 points, then give each tank an order.
item: Fight | Press Start battle. Drive your command tank with WASD or the arrows, aim with the pointer and hold Space or click to fire. Your command tank must survive.
item: Command under fire | Press O for Command mode: time runs at half speed and your gun is off while you send tanks to new squares. F, R and E order fall in, retreat and advance.
item: Win | Capture the enemy HQ, hold the contested crossroads, or eliminate their platoon.
[/features]

[features grid]
eyebrow: CONTROLS
heading: Controls
item: Drive | WASD or the arrow keys. On phones, the analog thumbstick.
item: Aim and fire | Aim with the pointer; hold Space, click or right-click to fire. On phones, aim goes to the nearest visible enemy: hold Fire.
item: Squad orders | F fall in, R retreat, E advance. On phones, the labeled buttons.
item: Pick a wingman | 1–9 or click a tank. On phones, tap the tank or its platoon key.
item: Command mode | O, then a tank and a square. Enter locks the order, Backspace clears it, N moves to the next tank and H attacks the HQ.
item: Pause and camera | P or the white flag pauses: Keep fighting or Surrender. M and drag pans, C recenters.
[/features]

[features rows]
eyebrow: MODES
heading: DOM Battlefield modes
item: Quick start | Anything you have not picked is rolled at random, and the battle starts.
item: Plan battle | Time stays frozen while three cards set your platoon, your loadout and every tank's route. Start battle when you are ready.
item: Four operations | Hedgerow breach, Desert spearhead, Winter crossroads and Twin bridges, each with Light, Normal or Heavy terrain and a dice for a new layout.
item: Three ways to win | Capture an opposing HQ, hold a contested crossroads, or eliminate a platoon.
[/features]

[features grid]
eyebrow: TIPS
heading: Tips
item: Retreat to repair | Retreat sends your squad back to headquarters, where tanks repair. Advance returns them to the objective.
item: Bend the route | Drag a route line or its flag to change where a tank goes. Every map has alternate connected routes.
item: Know your cover | Houses, hedges and parked cars break into debris and rubble. Rocks and water are permanent; fences stop tanks but let shells through.
item: True full screen on phones | On iPhone, Add to Home Screen; on Android, Install. The first visit on a phone explains how.
[/features]

[faq]
Q: Is DOM Battlefield free to play?
A: Yes. It plays in your browser with no install and no account, and it is open source (MIT).
Q: Can I play DOM Battlefield on a phone?
A: Yes. Phone battles put a see-through rail on each side of a full-screen field, with an analog thumbstick and labeled buttons.
Q: How is it related to Sherman Bytes?
A: Same tanks: Sherman Bytes draws every tank with its own sprite code. DOM Battlefield adds zero bytes to the Sherman game.
Q: Is there a two-player mode?
A: Not in DOM Battlefield yet. Battlefield Blitz, its arcade version, has a two-player mode.
[/faq]
