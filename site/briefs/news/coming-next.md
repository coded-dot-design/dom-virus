Battlefield Blitz, the first of the new cartridges, has shipped. Next on the bench: DOM Crossfire, DOM Kaiju and DOM Flock, plus multiplayer Battlefield. All of them are planned, not promised. The bar for every one: the explosions on our home page and the tanks wrecking the DOM Battlefield site.

[text]
heading: Shipped: Battlefield Blitz

The Blitz version of DOM Battlefield is out. Free for all, every tank parachutes in, and most kills in three minutes wins.

It is the first game built on Fizlet, the shared engine.
[/text]

[features rows]
heading: Planned games | In build order.
item: DOM Crossfire | Planned, and next up. The DOM VIRUS hero meets the Battlefield arena. Fly the interceptor or drive the tank, and swap any time while the other one wings for you.
item: DOM Kaiju | Planned. A giant pixel gorilla falls onto your page and climbs it from the footer to the top, smashing as he goes. Text catches fire, people fall, choppers swarm in. | See DOM Kaiju -> /games/kaiju/
item: DOM Flock | Planned, still an idea. Pixel birds burst out of the page in pairs. You are the crosshair, and hit birds drop into the text.
[/features]

[text]
heading: Kaiju's numbers are targets

DOM Kaiju is planned as two builds: an embed for any page and a full board. One page is one level.

The embed's size target is 30 kB gzip or less. It is a target, not a measurement.
[/text]

[text]
heading: Multiplayer Battlefield

Multiplayer is planned: two players, two pages, one battle, with rooms, co-op and one-on-one.
[/text]

[text]
heading: One engine under every cartridge

The games will share one small engine, Fizlet: one loop, one way to read the page and give it back, one set of keys.

Each cartridge gets an engine cut next: less code, the same feel, at least as fast as today. The old builds stay playable.
[/text]
