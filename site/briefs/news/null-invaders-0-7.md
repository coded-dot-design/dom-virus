Null Invaders 0.7 adds sound and a proper phone cockpit: an analog joystick and a round Fire button you can hold while you fly. Null Invaders is the shooter where you fly an interceptor through a real webpage, turn letters into binary and break the button factories.

[text]
heading: What's new for players

Firing, hits, weapon upgrades and new waves now have synthesized sound cues.

On phones, the arrow pad becomes an analog joystick with eight-way flight. Fire is the round crosshair button from DOM Battlefield, with a 3D press. Fly and hold Fire together.

The scoreboard shares its controls with the other games: sound, pause or resume, minimize and Recall.

Pulse, Twin, Trident and Overdrive still unlock as you earn combat XP. Destroyed factories still stop spawning for the rest of the wave.
[/text]

[text]
heading: For developers

The controls come from the shared DOM VIRUS runtime, snapshot 0.21.0. The gzip budget grew by 2 KB to pay for them and now sits at 24,000 bytes.

Setup, configuration and integration examples live in the repository README.
[/text]
