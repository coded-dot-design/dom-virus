DOM VIRUS is open: an arcade of six free browser games by Chris Cooley. Four play right on the page you are reading and turn its letters and buttons into the game board. Two Battlefield tank games run full screen on their own sites. No install, no account, and every game is open source (MIT).

[features rows]
heading: Play on the page | Four arcade games, one webpage.
item: Sherman Bytes | Drive a pixel tank across the page: breach the button bases, build cover and hold the page against siege waves. | Play Sherman Bytes -> /games/sherman-bytes/
item: Swarm Protocol | Bugs eat the letters into binary and drain your buttons. Squash them before the interface falls apart. | Play Swarm Protocol -> /games/swarm-protocol/
item: Byte Runner | A pixel frog races food pipes in the page's whitespace, eats the real text and hunts the flies. | Play Byte Runner -> /games/byte-runner/
item: Null Invaders | Fly an interceptor through the page, shoot letters into binary and break the button factories over four waves. | Play Null Invaders -> /games/null-invaders/
[/features]

[features rows]
heading: Board games | Full screen, on their own sites.
item: DOM Battlefield | Squad tactics: freeze time to plan every route for up to twelve tanks, then command them under fire. | Play DOM Battlefield -> /games/battlefield/
item: Battlefield Blitz | The arcade version: parachute in, call airstrikes, most kills in three minutes wins. New this week: a real two-player lobby. | Play Battlefield Blitz -> /games/battlefield-blitz/
[/features]

[text]
heading: Recall gives the page back

Every game that plays on the page ends the same way. Press Escape or Recall, and the game removes what it added and restores what it changed and still owns. The page is the board, then it is the page again.
[/text]

[text]
heading: Built on Fizlet

Five of the six games run on Fizlet, Chris Cooley's micro animation and game engine for the DOM. The engine story, its bricks and its measured budgets live on [fizlet.com](https://fizlet.com/).
[/text]
