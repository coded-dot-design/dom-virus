DOM VIRUS launches with ten tiny arcade games that play on a real webpage. Letters become points, buttons become enemy factories, and Recall gives the page back. No install to play. Every game is open source under the MIT licence.

[text]
heading: One maker, a game a week

DOM VIRUS is made by one person, Chris Cooley, a game a week on the side.

Play it here, then make it part of your own project. Visit GitHub for the source code, setup instructions, and integration examples.
[/text]
