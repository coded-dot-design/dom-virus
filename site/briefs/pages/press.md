DOM VIRUS is a family of tiny open-source arcade games that play on a real webpage. The DOM is the game board: letters become points and buttons become enemy factories. Hit Recall to give the page back. By Chris Cooley · coded.design.

[stats]
heading: Fact sheet
6 | games playable today: four on the page, two board games
MIT | licence for every game, the engine and the site
22.9 to 34.6 kB | gzip size of each embedded game, engine included
2.8 kB | Fizlet engine kernel, gzip, against a 4 KB budget
0 | runtime dependencies in the game packages
[/stats]

[text]
heading: About the games
Each embedded game plays across the page you are reading, then gives it back on Recall. No install to play.

DOM Battlefield and Battlefield Blitz are full board games built from Sherman Bytes, played on their own site.

Battlefield Blitz runs on Fizlet, the family's small engine. The embedded games move onto it next.
[/text]
