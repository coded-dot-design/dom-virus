heading: What the games do to a page
The games run in your browser and play on the page you have open. While you play, they change that page: hits can turn letters into 0 and 1, and buttons become enemy bases.

Recall gives back what the game changed and still owns. If something else on the page changed the same part since, that change stays.

The games change only what your browser shows. They do not touch the website's server, its files or anyone else's copy of the page.
